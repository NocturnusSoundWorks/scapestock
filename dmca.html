Copyright & DMCA Policy

ScapeStock respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of this website to do the same.

If you believe that any photo available on ScapeStock infringes your copyright or other intellectual property rights, you may submit a removal request according to the procedures described below.

Submitting a Copyright Removal Request

To request the removal of copyrighted material from ScapeStock, please send a notice containing the following information:

  • Your full name and contact information
  • Your email address
  • A description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed
  • The exact URL of the material on ScapeStock that you believe is infringing
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner
  • Your electronic or physical signature

Review Process

Upon receiving a valid copyright complaint, we will review the request and may remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing material.

We may also contact the uploader or relevant parties to investigate the claim if necessary.

False Claims

Submitting false or misleading copyright claims may result in legal liability.

Please ensure that you are the rightful copyright holder or authorized representative before submitting a removal request.

Third-Party Content

ScapeStock hosts photographs provided by creators. While we strive to ensure that all uploaded materials respect copyright laws, we cannot guarantee that all content is free from third-party rights.

If you believe your work has been used improperly, please notify us so we can promptly investigate and take appropriate action.
